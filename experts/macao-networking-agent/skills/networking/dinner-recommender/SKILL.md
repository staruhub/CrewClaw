# Dinner Recommender

## Overview

Recommend Macao dinner, coffee, or quiet meeting venues that fit the relationship stage, conversation goal, and travel constraints.

## When to Use

Use when the user wants to invite a lead to dinner, coffee, drinks, or a calm follow-up meeting in Macao.

## Workflow

1. Identify the relationship stage, formality, budget, location constraints, and conversation goal.
2. Suggest 2-4 venue styles or specific venues when the user has provided enough context.
3. Explain why each option fits the meeting and what to confirm before booking.
4. Include a short invitation line the user can review and send manually.
