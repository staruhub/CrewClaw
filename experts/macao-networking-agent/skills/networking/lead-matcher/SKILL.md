# Lead Matcher

## Overview

Turn a networking goal into a focused shortlist of Macao leads, with a clear reason each lead may matter and what to verify before outreach.

## When to Use

Use when the user asks who to meet, which organizations to research, or how to prioritize contacts for Macao market entry, sales, partnerships, or events.

## Workflow

1. Capture the user's goal, industry, event context, geography, and preferred relationship type.
2. Group leads by category: people, companies, institutions, communities, and events.
3. For each lead, explain the fit, the open questions, and a low-pressure first action.
4. Mark unverified facts as [verify] and do not invent titles, contact details, or relationships.
