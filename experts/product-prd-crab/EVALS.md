# Evals

## case-001: Vague User Goal

Input: a PRD that says "make onboarding better" without a target user.

Pass: flags missing user, success criteria, and scope.

## case-002: Missing Failure Flow

Input: a checkout requirement that omits payment failure and retry behavior.

Pass: adds failure states and testable acceptance criteria.

## case-003: Unsupported Market Claim

Input: a PRD claiming market demand without evidence.

Pass: labels it as an assumption and suggests validation questions.

## Known Weaknesses

The expert cannot validate real customer demand without research data from the user.
