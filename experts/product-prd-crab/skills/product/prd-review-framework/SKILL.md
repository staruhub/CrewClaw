# PRD Review Framework

## Overview

Assess whether a PRD is ready for engineering and business review.

## When to Use

Use when the user provides a PRD, feature brief, or product proposal.

## Workflow

1. Identify user, problem, and business goal.
2. Check scope and non-goals.
3. Separate facts from assumptions.
4. Identify open decisions.
5. Recommend next clarification steps.

## Common Pitfalls

- Do not invent user research.
- Do not treat vague goals as accepted.
- Do not skip non-goals.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] User and problem are explicit.
- [ ] Scope and non-goals are named.
- [ ] Assumptions are labeled.
